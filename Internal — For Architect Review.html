01 — Context

What This Project Is

In one sentence: a single web + mobile platform that lets a Project Management Consultant track daily construction progress, schedule, compliance and money across 56 solar sites run by 20 different contractors — replacing spreadsheets and WhatsApp updates with one live source of truth.

Who it's for: a Project Management Consultancy (PBR Enterprises) overseeing a government solar programme, the main EPC contractor who owns the outcome (Balaji Human Safety), the 20 sub-contractors physically building the sites, and the finance teams releasing payment against verified work.

What it actually does, mechanically: every site engineer logs a daily report from the field (with GPS-tagged photos). Those reports roll up automatically into weekly/monthly summaries and into a live schedule (a CPM/Gantt engine) that flags when any site is at risk of slipping. Government approvals and technical certifications are tracked per site with expiry alerts. Payment is only released once a physical measurement is jointly certified against the invoice raised. Four different people looking at the same underlying data — a PMC officer, a site contractor, a client executive, and a finance controller — each see only the slice relevant to their job.

Why it needs to exist: at 56 concurrent sites with hard government deadlines and milestone-linked payments, delay or fraud anywhere is expensive and currently invisible until it's already a problem. The system's job is to surface risk (schedule, compliance, financial) as early as possible, and to make sure money only moves when real work is verified.

06 — Behaviour

Key User Flows

Four flows that touch every module and every role — worth whiteboarding before backend design starts.

FLOW 1Daily report → approval → aggregation

Site Engineer submits DPR (mobile, offline-capable)→ Auto-sync when online→ PMC reviews in queue→ Approve / Reject / Return→ Approved DPRs roll into WPR/MPR automatically

FLOW 2Schedule delay → escalation

Task marked delayed on a site's Gantt→ CPM engine recalculates critical path→ Threshold breached (e.g. >3 days)→ Alert fires: EPC → PMC → Client→ District health indicator turns Amber/Red

FLOW 3Invoice → verified payment

EPC submits RA bill against a milestone→ Field measurement logged (JMC)→ PMC + Finance jointly certify→ Finance approves payment→ Contractor ledger updated

FLOW 4Compliance expiry → renewal

System tracks ComplianceItem expiry date→ Advance-notice alert to Site Engineer + PMC→ Renewed document uploaded→ PMC verifies & updates status→ Site heat-map cell returns to Green

08 — Guardrails

Constraints for Architecture

The handful of non-functional requirements that will actually shape technical decisions, not the full list.

Offline-first mobile

DPR entry must work with zero connectivity and sync later — this drives local storage + conflict resolution design on the mobile app, not just a "nice to have" sync button.

RBAC at the API layer

Site-scoping for EPC users must be enforced server-side on every endpoint, not just hidden in the UI — a leaked EPC login should never expose another contractor's data.

Geo/time-stamped media

Every photo/video needs GPS + server-verified timestamp captured at upload, with geofence validation against the site's registered radius.

Concurrent scale

~150–200 total users across 56 sites and 20 EPCs, with peak concurrent writes during evening DPR submission windows.

Financial data integrity

Invoice → JMC → payment must be an auditable, append-only chain — no silent edits to a certified measurement or an approved invoice.

Encryption & audit

TLS in transit, encryption at rest for documents and financial records, full audit log on every write to sensitive entities.
