SEC. 01

Executive Summary & Our Understanding

Balaji Human Safety has been awarded a 1,810 MW decentralized solar power programme under the Government of Maharashtra's PM‑KUSUM scheme, spanning 14 districts and 56 individual site locations, executed through 20 EPC sub‑contractors and overseen by PBR Enterprises as Project Management Consultant.

The scale and distributed nature of this programme — dozens of concurrent construction fronts, multiple government clearances per site, milestone‑linked payments, and a hard Commercial Operation Date — cannot be reliably governed through spreadsheets and WhatsApp updates. It requires a single cloud platform that gives every stakeholder, from a site engineer to a government‑facing executive, the exact slice of real‑time truth they need.

This document is our detailed, ready‑to‑execute scope of work. It translates your concept note into concrete modules, screens, data fields, workflows, and a delivery plan — engineered specifically around four functional pillars (Progress Reporting, CPM Scheduling, Technical & Compliance, Finance & Accounts) and four role‑based dashboards (PMC, EPC, Main Contractor, Finance).

Nothing in the concept note has been left unaddressed; where the brief implied a need without spelling out the mechanics — approval workflows, audit trails, escalation logic, offline sync — we have made it explicit below so it can be reviewed, sized, and signed off in one pass.

SEC. 03

Detailed Functional Scope

Four functional modules, each expanded from the concept note into the specific screens, fields, and workflow states required to build and test it without further back‑and‑forth.

MOD A

Progress Reporting & Documentation

A.1 Daily Progress Report (DPR)

  • Structured, mandatory daily entry per site: piles driven, structures erected, modules mounted, cable trenching (RM), earthing & cabling status, labour headcount by trade (skilled / unskilled / supervisory), machinery deployed and idle‑hours, local weather and hours lost to it, material received, and safety observations or incidents.
  • Photo and video upload against each activity line, geo‑ and time‑stamped at capture (see Section 05).
  • Configurable reminder notifications to site engineers for same‑day submission, with a missed‑DPR flag visible to the PMC.
  • Offline‑first form so entries made without network coverage are saved locally and synced automatically once connectivity resumes.

A.2 Weekly & Monthly Progress Reports (WPR / MPR)

  • Automatic roll‑up of DPR data by week and month, at site, district, contractor and programme level — no manual re‑entry.
  • Consolidated photo/video gallery per reporting period, with planned‑vs‑actual progress percentage displayed alongside.
  • One‑click generation of a formatted, exportable WPR/MPR document ready for circulation.

A.3 Quarterly Milestone Audits

  • Executive summary view comparing planned vs. actual progress across all levels of the hierarchy for the quarter.
  • Variance and deviation analysis with a corrective‑action tracker: owner, target date, and closure status per open item.

A.4 Review & Approval Workflow

  • Every DPR/WPR passes through Submit → PMC Review → Approve / Reject / Return‑for‑Correction → Resubmit, with a visible status and full revision history.
  • Comment threads attached to each submission; configurable SLA timers that escalate a report sitting in "pending review" beyond a set number of days.
MOD B

CPM Scheduling & Monitoring Engine

B.1 Interactive Gantt Chart

  • Task and sub‑task hierarchy per site following the standard build sequence: site mobilization → civil works → structure mounting → module installation → inverter/transformer installation & testing → grid synchronisation → COD.
  • Task dependencies (finish‑to‑start and others), durations, and resource assignments; drag‑and‑drop rescheduling that automatically shifts dependent tasks.

B.2 Critical Path & Early Warning System

  • Automatic critical‑path calculation and visual highlighting across the full 56‑site network, rolling up to a single programme‑level critical path to COD.
  • Configurable delay thresholds (e.g. >3 days slip on a critical task) trigger multi‑channel alerts — in‑app, email, and SMS/WhatsApp — with an escalation matrix from Site Engineer → EPC PM → PMC → Client based on severity and duration.

B.3 Baseline vs. Actual Tracking

  • Baseline schedule locked per milestone, with version‑controlled, approval‑gated re‑baselining when scope genuinely changes.
  • Schedule variance reporting per site, district and programme, so a delayed critical task is traceable to its exact origin.
MOD C

Technical & Compliance Vault

C.1 Approved Vendor List (AVL) & Spec Compatibility

  • Master AVL database (module make and efficiency rating, inverter make and capacity, structure vendor, cabling specification, etc.).
  • Automatic check‑and‑balance validation whenever a site engineer logs material used, with non‑conformance flagged to the PMC instantly rather than at the next audit.

C.2 Document & Drawing Repository

  • Version‑controlled central vault for drawings, single‑line diagrams, test certificates and datasheets, tagged by site and discipline with full‑text search.
  • Access restricted by role, so an EPC only sees documents relevant to its assigned sites.

C.3 Government & Regulatory Compliance Checklist

  • Per‑site checklist tracking land approvals, MSEDCL/MAHAGENCO clearances, CEIG safety certification, and net‑metering registration status.
  • Expiry and renewal tracking with advance‑notice alerts, rolling up into a Red/Amber/Green compliance heat‑map per site and district.

C.4 Quality & Inspection Reports

  • Digitised QA/QC checklists and test‑report uploads (module flash test, cable insulation, earthing resistance, etc.).
  • Non‑Conformance Report (NCR) tracker with an owner and a closure workflow, mirroring the DPR approval pattern for consistency.
MOD D

Financial & Accounts Suite

D.1 Milestone‑Based Invoicing

  • Billing triggered strictly against verified physical completion percentage per milestone, with a Running Account (RA) bill submission portal for each EPC.

D.2 Joint Measurement Certification (JMC)

  • Field measurement entry → PMC joint verification → certification, with the certified JMC linked directly to invoice release so payment always matches verified physical work.

D.3 Contractor Ledger

  • Advance payment tracking, retention‑money accounting, and automatic penalty / liquidated‑damages computation for delays against the CPM baseline.
  • Vendor‑ and contractor‑wise running ledger statement, exportable on demand.

D.4 GST & Tax Compliance Tracking

  • Per‑site and per‑invoice GST tracking with a live tax‑compliance status dashboard.

D.5 Budget vs. Actual & Financial MIS

  • Consolidated budget utilisation (committed vs. spent vs. balance) at project, district and site level, exportable as management‑ and government‑ready MIS reports.

SEC. 05

Cross‑Cutting Technical Requirements

Requirements that apply across every module rather than sitting inside one — the platform's field‑readiness, integrity, and security posture.

01Mobile‑First / Field Optimisation

  • Portal must load usably on 3G/4G at remote sites, with an app‑shell architecture and aggressive asset compression
  • Offline‑save capability for DPR forms with automatic background sync on reconnect
  • Conflict resolution for entries edited offline on more than one device

02Geofencing & Media Authentication

  • Every site photo/video upload captures GPS coordinates and a server‑verified timestamp at point of capture
  • Geofence validation flags a photo taken outside the registered site radius, reducing fraudulent reporting
  • Immutable audit trail linking each media file to the submitting user and device

03Security

  • SSL/TLS in transit; AES‑256 encryption at rest for all documents and financial data
  • OAuth2 / JWT‑based authentication, with RBAC enforced at the API layer — never in the UI alone
  • Full audit logging of reads and writes on sensitive records (financial, compliance)

04Notifications & Alerting

  • Multi‑channel delivery — in‑app, email, and SMS/WhatsApp Business API — for approvals, delay alerts and compliance expiries
  • User‑configurable notification preferences per role

05Scalability & Reliability

  • Cloud‑hosted, horizontally scalable to concurrent usage from 20 EPC teams and 56 sites without degraded field‑app performance
  • Automated daily backups with a defined recovery‑point objective for disaster recovery

06Reporting & Data Portability

  • Exportable PDF/Excel reports at every level of the hierarchy for government and internal circulation
  • Structured data export (CSV/API) so historical data is never locked into the platform
SEC. 06

Proposed Technology Stack

A stack chosen for exactly this brief's constraints: unreliable field connectivity, high concurrent write volume from 56 sites, and government‑grade security expectations.

Web Application

Admin & Dashboard Front‑End

  • React.js + TypeScript
  • Recharts / Chart.js for KPI & CPM visualisations
  • Google Maps API for the 56‑site geo view
Field Application

Mobile App (Android / iOS)

  • Cross‑platform build (Flutter or React Native)
  • Offline‑first local storage with background sync
  • Native camera + GPS for geo‑tagged media capture
Backend

API & Business Logic

  • .NET Core Web API, microservices per module (Progress, Scheduling, Compliance, Finance)
  • SignalR for real‑time delay & approval alerts
  • REST APIs with OAuth2 / JWT authentication
Data Layer

Storage

  • PostgreSQL / SQL Server as the primary relational store
  • Redis for caching & session management
  • Cloud object storage (Azure Blob / AWS S3) for photos, videos, drawings and certificates, served via CDN
Cloud & DevOps

Infrastructure

  • Containerised deployment (Docker) on Azure or AWS
  • CI/CD pipeline with staged environments (dev / UAT / production)
  • Auto‑scaling and geo‑redundant backup for disaster recovery
Integrations

Third‑Party Services

  • Twilio / WhatsApp Business API for field alerts
  • SMTP for email notifications & report delivery
  • Google Maps Geocoding API for geofence validation
Optional future enhancement (not in base scope): an AI‑assisted early‑warning layer that learns from historical DPR and CPM data to flag at‑risk sites before a threshold breach occurs — a natural Phase 4+ extension once 6–9 months of field data has accumulated.

SEC. 10

Assumptions, Dependencies & Exclusions

Stated up front so the scope above can be reviewed and priced without hidden surprises on either side.

Client‑Side Dependencies

  • Finalised master data: the list of 56 sites with coordinates, the 20 EPC contractors and their site assignments, the AVL master list, and the initial user/role list for RBAC provisioning.
  • Timely nomination of a single point of contact each from PBR and Balaji for UAT sign‑off at every phase gate.
  • Field devices (smartphones/tablets) and basic network connectivity at each site — assumed client‑provided, not part of this scope.

Out of Scope (this phase)

  • Direct system‑to‑system integration with government portals (MSEDCL/MAHAGENCO/CEIG) unless official APIs are made available — scope is limited to manual status tracking and document upload until then.
  • Migration of any pre‑existing historical progress data — to be scoped separately once the source format is known.
  • Large‑scale end‑user training rollout across all 56 sites — train‑the‑trainer sessions for PMC/EPC lead users are included; broader rollout to be planned jointly.
Third‑party API costs (SMS/WhatsApp, maps, cloud hosting, storage) are billed at actuals and are not included in the development quotation. One structured round of UAT‑driven revisions is included per phase; further changes follow a standard change‑order process.
